## Design Read and competing directions

**British local building contractor · blueprint order with tactile masonry · brand register · SOUL 7 / SPECTACLE 2 / DENSITY 5 · static image engine.** Complementary dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 6, MOTION_INTENSITY 2, VISUAL_DENSITY 5.

A — selected: cool mineral background, blueprint navy, condensed workmanlike display type; asymmetric split hero; offset material service pair; a blue lower repair/proof section; a compact contact ledger. It reads as a practical skilled local business. Unique material studies add depth without inventing project proof.

B — rejected: fullbleed dark-blue architectural campaign, giant white statement, horizontal material atlas, quote banner and contact sheet. It is a materially different composition but overstates a modest local contractor as a premium architectural practice and gives less first-screen service/contact clarity. No need for a high-spectacle engine.

Named lazy defaults rejected: generic smiling-worker stock hero, beige luxury-craft template, identical three-card feature grid, orange emergency-trade buttons, fake trust logos, repeated cropped hero images and giant empty closing slogan. Soul comes from the source card's blue/architectural cue, the actual range of masonry/outdoor/repair work, and specific dated feedback. Not a reused customer template or brand asset.

Tokens: --paper #edf1f2, --ink #182d3b, --blue #174766, --muted #526472; translucent blue borders; uniform 3px radius. Display Barlow Condensed Bold reflects practical signage/material catalogues; body Barlow Regular/Semibold provides approachable legibility. SIL OFL local files. Light theme locked because mineral studies and blue service band form one authored composition. No auto animation, hover/focus/active only; reduced-motion removes smooth scrolling/transitions.

Premium substrate: tinted neutrals; static subtle SVG turbulence overlay at 0.025 opacity on key blue surface, pointer-events none; deliberate text/image z-order; no fake decorative grid. Display tracking -0.025em and line-height .94; no headline clipping. Layouts: split hero, staggered image features, editorial repair/quote band, contact ledger. Minimal grain serves material tactility, not an engine.
